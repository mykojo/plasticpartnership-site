Smart Buying

Buy by Yield, Not by Cost

Work smarter — not harder — by factoring in yield and equivalent cost to your buying decisions.

Why It Matters

Yield to Cost Calculators

Hundreds of studies have concluded that when manufacturers buy solely on cost it always results in negative unintended outcomes. At Plastic Partnership we understand why some manufacturers focus on cost, especially when dealing with mixed material from curbside schemes and landfills.

At Plastic Partnership our collection network intercepts 100% of our post consumer PET bottles at source, from the environment, before it reaches a landfill. We offer our customers a guarantee that the target material (PET composition) yield they receive from our bales is a minimum of 95%.

The Cost of Cutting Corners

Why Buying Solely on Cost Costs More

📉

Lower Quality Products

Cheaper materials lead to compromised product quality, customer complaints, returns, and a damaged reputation.

🔧

Higher Defect Rates

Cheaper materials are more prone to failure — increasing costs through rework, repairs, and returns.

⏱️

Production Downtime

Lower quality inputs lead to increased production downtime — missed deadlines, delayed shipments, and lost revenue.

😞

Reduced Customer Satisfaction

When cost is prioritised over quality, the end customer suffers — leading to negative reviews and decreased loyalty.
