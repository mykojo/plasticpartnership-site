Our Supply Chain

Micro-Suppliers, Maximum Quality

By supporting a small army of micro-suppliers we ensure our incoming material stream is from the environment — not landfills. Our quality control system includes 3 checkpoints:

1

On Delivery to Our Collection Centre

Every delivery is checked on arrival. Non-PET materials are identified and separated before the material passes to the core sorting team.

2

At Our Secondary Sorting Section

Experienced sorters rapidly recognise and remove any non-PET materials that were not intercepted at delivery.

3

At the Conveyor

Additional quality control specialists work at our heavy-duty conveyor — a final check before baling to catch anything that passed the first two stages.

By paying attention to detail and educating our entire supply chain, we guarantee a 95% target material yield to our customers. Try our yield calculator to find the equivalency between your current material yield and ours.

The Business Case

Why Yield Produces Better Outcomes Over Cost

📉

Lower Quality Products

The most obvious consequence of buying on cost alone is that product quality is compromised — leading to customer complaints, returns, and a damaged reputation.

❌

Higher Defect Rates

Choosing suppliers based on cheapest materials leads to a higher defect rate in finished products — increasing costs through rework, repairs, and returns.

⏳

Production Downtime

Lower quality materials are more prone to failure or damage — resulting in increased downtime, missed deadlines, delayed shipments, and lost revenue.

📊

Higher Long-Term Costs

Although buying on cost may seem like saving money short term, replacing defective products and managing customer complaints leads to increased costs in the long run.
