Core Product

Our High Density PET Bales

Our core product is high density bales of PET. Our product includes labels, collars and caps.

  • 80% of material sourced from within 5km of the Ocean
  • Balance collected from within 10km of the Ocean
  • Sorting and quality control performed manually
  • 95% estimated target material (PET) yield
  • Majority consists of clear and light blue PET water bottles
  • None of our bottles are derived from landfills or domestic mixed curbside collection

Quality Control

3 Layers of Quality Control

All PET materials undergo 3 layers of sorting and quality control screening before being baled.

1

On Delivery — Arrivals Check

Deliveries from our collectors can include unwanted materials. All materials are quality checked on arrival. Non-PET materials are separated out, and the material is then passed to our core sorting team.

2

Core Sorting Team

Our experienced core sorting team rapidly recognise and remove any non-PET materials not intercepted by the arrivals process. Their speed and accuracy are reinforced through incentive programmes.

3

Conveyor Quality Control

Additional quality control specialists work at our heavy duty conveyor, diligently removing any unwanted materials that may have passed our first two processes.

Our Approach

Continuous Improvement — Kaizen

Plastic Partnership uses Kaizen techniques to continuously improve our processes. Our way of working involves inspecting and rapidly adapting — we embrace change as an opportunity.

All of our staff are invested in helping our organisation improve. We endeavour to provide our customers with the very best quality, high-yield post consumer PET.
