About PET

Plastic — An Amazing Persistent Product

Plastic bottles are made from polyethylene terephthalate — PET for short. Globally, over 2 billion people still do not have access to clean drinking water, according to the United Nations, and bottled water is often the only safe option.

As a result, single use plastic bottles have become one of the world's worst contaminants. It takes up to 450 years for a bottle to decompose — and when it does, it becomes microplastic, entering our bodies through the food chain.

According to the World Economic Forum, more than 90% of plastic is never recycled, and 8 million metric tons of plastic waste are dumped into the oceans annually. At this rate, there will be more plastic than fish in the world's oceans by 2050.

Ghana Specifically

Ghana's Unique Plastic Challenge

🚿

Unsafe Drinking Water

Ghana's water can be unsafe to drink. As a result, the majority of its 30 million population drink daily from single use plastic bottles — creating a massive waste problem.

🗑️

2,000 Tons Daily

Accra alone generates over 2,000 tons of plastic waste daily. With a lack of effective municipal waste collection, much ends up in waterways, the ocean, landfills — or is burned.

🌍

450 Years to Decompose

Post consumer plastic is one of the most durable substances made by man. Left alone it takes up to 450 years to decompose — becoming microplastic that enters our food chain.

Our Solution

What We Do About It

Plastic Partnership works with individual waste pickers, middlemen, the hospitality industry and schools to prevent plastic bottles entering the ocean.

100% of our product is collected from within 5km of the Ocean and is classed as Ocean Bound Plastic — one of the most environmentally significant classifications in the industry.

  • Collect from within 5km of the ocean
  • Three-stage quality control process
  • High density baling for efficient export
  • Support for hundreds of waste picker jobs
  • App-tracked supply chain transparency
