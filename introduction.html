An Introduction to Plastic Partnership

Everything you need to know about who we are and why we do what we do.

What We Do

Very High Quality PET Bales from Ghana

Plastic Partnership produces very high quality, 95/5 or greater PET bales consisting of clear and light blue post consumer water and carbonated drink bottles that have been collected from within 5km of the ocean.

We do not collect from curbside schemes and landfills. Every bottle in our bales comes directly from the environment — documented, tracked and verified.

The Problem

Ghana's Water Problem Creates a Plastic Crisis

Ghana's water can be unsafe to drink and as a result, the majority of its 30 million population drink daily from single use plastic bottles. Accra, the capital, generates over 2,000 tons of plastic waste daily — and with a lack of effective municipal waste collection, a lot ends up in waterways, the ocean and landfills or is burned.

Traceability

Our Materials Do Not Come From Landfills

We've developed an app that enables us to track exactly where our supply comes from: 81% from within 5km of the ocean, 100% from within 30km. As a plastics manufacturer, if your customers care about source of supply, you'll find us the ideal supplier.

Social Responsibility

We Are Socially Responsible

🎓

Education Programmes

We fund a 1,000-girl conference and provide a speaker to talk about reducing, reusing and recycling. We provide musical instruments, laptops and books to schools in exchange for bottles collected by the school and pupils.

👶

Employee-First Policies

We created a day care facility so employees with children can rotate between working and caring for other employees' children. All employees who leave keep their company-funded PPE, boots and T-shirts.
