Plastic Partnership Timeline

270 Days from Idea to Production

Using Agile techniques, we moved from concept to a fully operational recycling facility faster than anyone thought possible.

Early 2022
The Idea
Three principals with different business backgrounds come together around a shared mission: prevent plastic from Ghana from entering the ocean, while creating sustainable jobs and income for local communities.
Mid 2022
Factory Secured & Renovation Begins
A disused factory in Tema is identified and secured. Significant investment begins to upgrade the 20,000 square metre site with new modern equipment and a high-throughput layout designed for scale.
Mid 2022
Supply Network Established
Work begins identifying, training and financially assisting local entrepreneurs to set up plastic bottle collection. Relationships formed with waste pickers, middlemen, hospitality industry and schools.
Late 2022
First Production
Plastic Partnership achieves first production — just 270 days from the original idea. Initial focus on completing Phase 1 of the factory while building volume capacity through process design.
November–December 2022
Process Design Phase
Focus on completing Phase 1 and establishing three-stage quality control processes. Staff hired and trained, living wages established with free daily meal and health plans.
Early 2023
Quality & Transparency Programme
Live inspection programme launched — customers worldwide can view the factory via high-definition Zoom sessions. Supply chain tracking app deployed to document the origin of all materials.
2023
Advisory Board Established
Industry experts Lanre Akinola, Kevin Mills and Michael V. Roberts join the Advisory Board, providing guidance on plastics industry, Africa expertise and entrepreneurship.
2024–2025
Scale & Community
Collector network grows to support over 115 third-party waste pickers. Educational programmes launched including 1,000-girl conference and school instrument programmes. GSUPER scheme planned for launch in Accra.
2025
World Environment Day Commitment
Plastic Partnership commits to expanding the collection network, partnering with local businesses on plastic-free alternatives, and launching the groundbreaking GSUPER (Ghana's Single-Use Plastic Environmental Recycling) scheme.
