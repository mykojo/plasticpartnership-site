Our Commitment

World Environment Day 2025 — Our Commitment

Plastic pollution is one of the most urgent environmental challenges of our time. At Plastic Partnership Ghana, we are committed to tackling this crisis by collecting, recycling, and repurposing plastic waste to create a cleaner, greener future.

🌍

Our Mission

We believe in a world where plastic waste is no longer a threat to our environment. We work to reduce plastic waste in Ghana's cities and waterways, promote sustainable recycling practices, and educate communities on responsible plastic disposal.

📣

2025 Theme: Ending Plastic Pollution

This year's World Environment Day theme calls for urgent action. We are stepping up by expanding our collection network to reach more communities, partnering with local businesses to promote plastic-free alternatives, and hosting educational workshops.

🏫

School Programmes

We employ a full-time teacher who visits schools in Accra helping them create recycling programs. We fund conferences and provide speakers — and supply musical instruments, laptops and books to schools in exchange for bottles collected.

Coming Soon

Get Ready for GSUPER

We will shortly be launching GSUPER — Ghana's Single-Use Plastic Environmental Recycling scheme — as part of our commitment to a cleaner environment and sustainable waste management.

This groundbreaking initiative ensures multiple Ghanaian entities actively recycle plastic waste. Plastic Partnership Limited will lead the charge, deploying our technology to efficiently manage post-consumer PET bottles.

  • Enhance operational efficiency across Ghana
  • Reduce environmental impact at scale
  • Create hundreds of new jobs
  • Leverage Ghana's Green Agenda and 24-hour economy

Plan: Launch in Accra in September 2025 — setting the stage for nationwide expansion. Want a 24-hour economy job? Check back and get ready to apply!
