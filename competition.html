Reusable Bag Competition

Design, Make, Win — celebrating the young entrepreneurs helping clean up Ghana.

The Competition

Design, Make, Win

A huge thank you to everyone who entered our schools bag competition. We are pleased to announce Georgina Irene Johnson of St Martin de Porres School as our prize winner, receiving GHc 2,500 for her winning entry.

Georgina has chosen to use her prize to help her brother with his entrepreneurial fish farm and treat herself to some new clothes. Georgina says: "Reduce, Reuse, Recycle" — well done Georgina!

In the coming months Georgina will be invited to bring her product to market and will share in the profits of the product!

Winning Criteria

  • The bag must look Awesomely Beautiful and be made by hand
  • It must be foldable and fit easily in a purse or pocket
  • It must be able to carry 1 day's worth of food for a family of 4
  • It must be strong enough to be reusable daily for over 1 year

Action has no season. We're cleaning up Ghana!

Community Impact

Plastic Partnership Works With Schools Across Ghana

We provide musical instruments, laptops and books to schools in exchange for bottles collected by the school and their pupils. We also fund a 1,000-girl conference and provide speakers on reducing, reusing and recycling.
