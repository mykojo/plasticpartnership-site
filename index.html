The Problem

The World Is Drowning in Plastic Waste

According to the World Economic Forum, more than 90% of plastic is never recycled, and a whopping 8 million metric tons of plastic waste are dumped into the oceans annually. At this rate, there will be more plastic than fish in the world's oceans by 2050.

What We Do

Preventing Plastic, Creating Jobs

Plastic Partnership is an organisation based in Ghana, West Africa that helps prevent plastic from entering the Ocean while supporting the creation of hundreds of good paying jobs.

We work with both established and new waste pickers — identifying, training and financially assisting local entrepreneurs in setting up collection. Using specialised machinery, the bottles are compressed into bales and sold to recycling organisations worldwide.

Overview

Communities Around the World Are Drowning in Post Consumer Plastic Waste

Plastic Partnership collects plastic bottles, compresses them into high density bales and delivers them to organisations that produce rPET for reuse in economies around the world.

♻️

Our Core Product

High Density PET bales consisting of post consumer plastic bottles picked by hand from metropolitan areas. None of our material is sourced from landfills.

🌊

100% Ocean Bound Plastic

We help reduce plastic entering the ocean by purposely collecting over 80% of our product from within 5km of the Ocean and documenting the source of all materials.

📦

Highest Quality Bales

Three layers of quality control screening before baling ensure a minimum 95% target material yield — consistently the highest in the industry.
