Our Mission

Preventing Plastic, Creating Opportunity

Plastic Partnership exists to prevent post consumer plastic entering the ocean while creating jobs and equal opportunities for the citizens of Ghana.

We set out with the goal of educating local communities on how to reduce consumption of plastic while learning to see it as a valuable commodity from which they can earn money.

Ghana's water can be unsafe to drink and as a result, the majority of its 30 million population drink daily from single use plastic bottles. Accra alone generates over 2,000 tons of plastic waste daily and with a lack of effective municipal curbside waste collection, a lot ends up in waterways, the ocean and landfills or is burned.

Working With Us

We Create Progressive, Sustainable Relationships

Plastic Partnership enjoys creating sustainable enjoyable relationships with its customers based on honesty and openness.

We Will Delight You If…

  • You value consistent, high-quality material
  • You care about the source and traceability of your supply
