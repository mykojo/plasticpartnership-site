How It All Started

Our story is of 3 principals, each with their own businesses, working selflessly and enthusiastically to create, in record time, a business that helps prevent plastic from Ghana from entering the ocean while playing its part in the greater circular economy.

In 2022 we started by furnishing a disused factory, adding new modern equipment and building a high throughput layout ready to scale. We used Agile principles to move fast — from idea to production in just 270 days.

We are proud to have created a business that provides a living wage for our employees, with a free daily meal and vocational health plans, while providing livelihoods for over 115 third party waste pickers.

Key Facts

Quick Facts

🏭

Purpose-Built Facility

Significant investment spent upgrading a 20,000 square metre factory providing room for expansion. Located close to Tema container port.

👥

People First

We pay our staff 4 times the minimum wage, support them with a daily meal and vocational health plans. Employees who leave keep their PPE, boots and T-shirts.

📍

100% Environment Sourced

We've developed an app to track exactly where our supply comes from. 81% from within 5km of the ocean, 100% from within 30km — none from landfills.

📹

Radical Transparency

We welcome customers to inspect our factory live via high definition cameras — from anywhere in the world on Zoom or Teams.

🎓

Education

We fund a 1,000-girl conference, provide speakers on recycling, and supply schools with musical instruments, laptops and books in exchange for bottles collected.

🌊

Ocean Bound Certified

100% of our product is classed as Ocean Bound Plastic — the most environmentally significant classification in the post-consumer plastic industry.
