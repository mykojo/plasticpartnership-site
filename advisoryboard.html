Meet the Team

Meet Our Advisory Board Members

As an organisation new to the industry, Plastic Partnership leans on a combination of industry knowledge, entrepreneurship and deep knowledge of Africa to help provide timely guidance.

Lanre Akinola

Climate Ambassador & Media Expert

Lanre Akinola, a climate ambassador for the Africa Climate Action Advocacy Initiative (ACAAI), has over 15 years of experience working in Africa-focused media and business. A respected commentator and thinker about the continent, Lanre has spoken on numerous international platforms including the World Economic Forum, the OECD, and the United Nations.

In addition to Bloomberg Media Initiative Africa, and serving as the former editor of African Business Magazine, Lanre was part of the founding team of the Financial Times' pioneering publication "This Is Africa" serving as editor from 2011–2015. Passionate about driving sustainable development in Africa, Lanre has previously served on the advisory board of Invest Africa and the Norwegian-African Business Association, and is a Desmond Tutu fellow.

Kevin Mills

Plastics Industry Expert

Kevin has a long and successful history in the Plastics Industry. Having owned and operated a successful extrusion business and one of the first to integrate recycled PET (rPET) into consumer products, Kevin's business consumed thousands of tonnes of recycled PET and was Prevented Ocean Plastic (POP) in particular.

Upon the successful sale of his business to a multinational, Kevin was delighted to accept a consultative role helping with the continued growth of the POP programme. He brings deep knowledge, authority and insights into the industry — enabling Plastic Partnership to benefit from his in-depth, detailed knowledge of the recycled plastics sector.

Michael V. Roberts

Entrepreneur & Business Builder

Michael V. Roberts has been turning the business world on its head since he began making deals in the 1970s. With his brother Steve, he started Roberts-Roberts & Associates. This led to opportunities in politics, real estate, broadcasting, wireless communication and construction. Michael, a former campaign manager for Jimmy Carter, was a regular guest at the White House.

His wide range of experience and entrepreneurism — including a history of building and selling successful companies including Roberts Wireless ($300m) and his $460 million thirty-four-company organisation — provides experience and critical over-the-horizon thinking to Plastic Partnership.
