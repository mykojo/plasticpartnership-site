Earn Money

Join the Fight and Earn Money

Become a collector and earn money. Whether you are an individual, a school or a business — you can earn money by collecting plastic bottles.

Earn Money Today
How Much Can I Earn?

We Pay GHc 100 for a Net of Plastic Bottles

Plastic Partnership pays 100 Cedis for a full net of clear and light blue plastic bottles (based on 125kg in weight).

What You'll Need to Get Started

  • A single fishing net — GHc 20
  • A pair of gloves
  • A place to store the net as it fills up

That's it. For just GHc 20 you can start earning money today while helping clean up Ghana.

Getting Started

How to Start Collecting

1

Register as a Collector

Call us to register as a collector. We pay you by mobile money ("Momo") — no bank account needed.

2

Find Your Bottles

Speak to restaurants, shops and households in your immediate area and arrange to collect their used bottles.

3

Fill Your Net

Fold the bottles to make them more compact and put them in a fishing net.

4

Deliver and Get Paid

Deliver the net to any of our collection centres where the nets are weighed. Get paid immediately and take away a new fishing net.

Growing Your Business

5 Nets / Day

When you build to 5 nets per day, Plastic Partnership will help you acquire a tricycle to make deliveries easier.

10 Nets / Day

At 10 nets per day, we'll help you acquire an electric tricycle to deliver faster.

30 Nets / Day

At greater volume, Plastic Partnership will help you set up your own collection centre where other collectors can contribute.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept any kind of plastic bottle?

All clear and light blue water bottles are accepted. We specifically do not accept brown malt beer bottles or green bottles.

My school wants to get involved — are there extra incentives?

Absolutely. Plastic Partnership works with schools to provide equipment ranging from musical instruments, blackboards and speakers, to uniforms for needy children and laptops for classes. Get in touch to learn more.

My business consumes a lot of water bottles — can you collect from us?

Yes. We will put you in touch with one of our collector network partners. The financial arrangement is left for you to negotiate directly with the collector.

How do you work with churches?

Churches are an important part of our society and we support the work they do in communities. If your church has more than 200 worshippers, we provide a collection skip where each family can bring their used water bottles. Every Tuesday our collection truck will collect the bottles, weigh them, and the church will receive mobile money on the same day.
